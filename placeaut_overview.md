# A calmer way to prepare for a new place

A visit can feel easier when your child has a clear idea of what will happen. New places bring unfamiliar rooms, sounds, people, and routines. For autistic children, that uncertainty can make an outing harder for the whole family.

[PlaceAut](https://placeaut.com/social-stories) helps Florida families find autism-friendly places, events, and resources where they can feel welcome and supported. One of its tools helps you prepare for a specific visit before you leave home. You choose a place, then create a social story that walks through the visit in a gentle, clear way.

A social story can be useful before a first visit, but it can also help when you are returning somewhere that was difficult last time. Having a simple plan to look at together may make the place feel less unfamiliar.

## Preparing for a visit with a social story

The PlaceAut Prepare Your Visit tool creates a personalized, step-by-step social story for the place you choose. It is free, printable, and shareable, so you can look at it with your child at home or share it with someone helping with the visit.

The story can help your child learn what to expect before you arrive. You can use it to talk about the place, the order of the visit, and the parts that may feel unfamiliar. Reading it together gives your child time to ask questions, notice details, and become more familiar with the plan.

Because the story is made for the place you select, it is more specific than a general explanation of going out. That can be helpful when your family is planning a trip to a new location or preparing for a change in the usual routine.

You do not have to read the whole story in one sitting. Try a few pages at a time, pause when your child wants to talk, and return to it before the visit. If your child likes to check information more than once, a printable copy gives them another way to revisit the plan.

## Finding what works for your family

PlaceAut Social Stories are one part of the tools available for families. The platform also helps families discover places and events, use Sensory Match, and find resources in Florida. Families can save places they want to remember and explore options that may suit their needs.

Parents can recommend places for review, which helps add more information for other families looking for somewhere to go. Businesses can list and manage their places on PlaceAut, giving families another way to find details while planning.

Before you leave home, think about what your child may want to know. You might talk about where you are going, who may be there, what may happen first, and what will happen when it is time to leave. A clear story cannot remove every surprise, but it can give your child a familiar way to understand the visit.

Preparing ahead, saving useful information, and sharing what you learn can make planning the next outing feel more manageable.
