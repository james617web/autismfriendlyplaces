# A Clearer Way to Show Families Your Place Is Ready

Finding somewhere to go with an autistic child can take more planning than it should. A place may look suitable online, but families still need to know what the visit will feel like, what support is available, and whether the business understands sensory needs.

That is why clear information matters. A simple sign can help a parent decide whether a place feels worth trying. It can also help a child prepare for a new setting before the visit.

## What the PlaceAut Autism-Friendly Badge Tells Families

The PlaceAut Badge Kit is for businesses that have been evaluated through real in-person family visits. It gives them a way to show that their place has been reviewed with family needs in mind.

This does not mean every visit will be the same for every child. Autism affects people in different ways, and each family knows what works best for them. The badge is one useful piece of information when parents are deciding where to go.

* **For families:** Seeing the badge can make it easier to find places that may be more welcoming and supportive. It can also give parents a starting point for asking about sensory details, the layout, busy times, events, and any other concerns before visiting.
* **For businesses:** The badge offers a clear way to share that their place has taken part in the PlaceAut evaluation process. Businesses can download the badge for use online and in print through the [PlaceAut Badge Kit](https://placeaut.com/badge-kit). The badge materials can be displayed where families are likely to see them before they visit.

## How Families Can Use the Information

A badge should be part of your planning, not a replacement for it. Before you go, look at the information available about the place and think about what your child may need. You might want to check whether there is a quiet area, how crowded the space can become, or whether staff can explain what to expect.

PlaceAut helps families in Florida discover places, events, social stories, and sensory matches. These tools can make it easier to compare options and prepare children for something unfamiliar. Parents can also recommend a place for review when they find somewhere that may help other families.

The goal is not to make every place feel the same. It is to make it easier for families to find useful information before they leave home, and to help businesses explain what they offer. A business using the PlaceAut autism-friendly badge is sharing one clear detail about how its place has been evaluated.

If you run a business, displaying the badge materials can answer an important question for families before they arrive: *has this place been looked at through real family visits?* If you are a parent, the badge can be one more detail to consider alongside your child’s individual needs.

A small amount of clear information can make trying a new place feel more manageable.
